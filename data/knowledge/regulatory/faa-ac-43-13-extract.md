# Advisory Circular 43.13 — paraphrased extract

> **Provenance.** This is a short paraphrase of publicly available FAA guidance,
> abridged for retrieval testing. It is not the complete document and must not be
> relied upon as the controlling text. The current AC is published by the FAA.

## Inspection intervals and continued airworthiness

Maintenance must be performed in accordance with methods, techniques and
practices acceptable to the Administrator. Where a manufacturer's maintenance
manual specifies a procedure or a limit, that procedure or limit governs.

## Condition monitoring programmes

Condition-monitored maintenance is permitted where the operator can demonstrate
that the monitoring programme reliably detects the deterioration mode before it
results in a functional failure. The programme must define:

- the parameters monitored,
- the alert thresholds,
- the corrective action associated with each threshold, and
- the means of verifying that the corrective action restored the condition.

## Record keeping

Maintenance records must identify the work performed, the date of completion, the
name of the person performing the work, and, where the work required approval for
return to service, the signature and certificate number of the approving person.

## Use of predictive analytics

Analytical tools may inform the scheduling of maintenance. They do not replace
the required inspections, and they do not by themselves constitute a
determination of airworthiness. A certificated person must make that
determination.
